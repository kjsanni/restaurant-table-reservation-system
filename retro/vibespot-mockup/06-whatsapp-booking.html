WhatsApp Booking Flow

1
Client opens chat Client Client sends a message to the restaurant’s WhatsApp Business number. Bot opens with menu options.
2
Client picks package Client Bot lists restaurant packages with price, duration, and included drinks. Client replies with a choice.
3
Client picks date & slot Client Bot checks real-time table availability and returns open time slots with table IDs and assigned staff.
4
Booking confirmed Bot Bot replies with a confirmation summary: date, time, table, assigned staff, total, and payment options.
5
Payment via WhatsApp Paystack Client can pay immediately or choose pay-at-restaurant. If paying now, bot sends a payment card in chat.
