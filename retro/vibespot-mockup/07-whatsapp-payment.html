WhatsApp Payment Flow

1
Bot offers payment options Bot After booking or on demand, the bot asks: pay via Mobile Money, Card, or at the restaurant.
2
Client picks provider Client MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, or AirtelTigo Money — supported under Ghana payment rails.
3
Bot sends Paystack payment card Paystack A payment card appears showing reservation amount, package, table, and a Pay button.
4
Client approves charge Client Client taps the button, enters PIN on their phone, and approves the charge.
5
Webhook confirmation Bot Paystack webhook confirms payment. Bot sends a receipt with transaction ID. Reservation status updates to Paid.
6
Deposits & partial payments Bot Enforce deposits for high-value packages. Bot collects deposit first, balance at restaurant or via a second link.
